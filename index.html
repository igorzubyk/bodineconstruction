About Bodine

People who assess
stand behind it.

We find where the water is coming from, explain the options clearly, and build the solution the property actually needs.

Bodine Construction Services, Inc. helps homeowners, real estate professionals, and property managers solve drainage and sewer repair problems across the Seattle area. Our crews bring current tools, sound judgment, and a practical plan to every project.

Many of our foremen, lead people, and office staff have worked with Bodine for 5–15 years; some for more than 25. That continuity is a commitment to our trade, our people, and the work we leave behind.
